## otrada-project.png

Use case: illustration-story. Create a refined painterly editorial illustration for the first three chapters of Uniform's Russian humorous detective story 'Возвращение «Отрады»'. This is contemporary fiction about online chat friends meeting in an apartment to redesign their chat. Scene: on a round dark wooden meeting table, a modern flat computer monitor shows a beautiful fictional castle on a high rocky hill above forest at blue twilight, conical tower roofs, a large moon behind several towers, subtle emerging stars. Castle must be clearly ONLY on the monitor, not a real medieval room. On the table: one modest USB flash drive, a faceted wooden pencil, a few porcelain tea cups. Quiet hints of empty chairs around table. Focus on the luminous screen and tiny flash drive; no faces, no text, no UI lettering, no logos. Medium: sophisticated illustrated detective novella, soft gouache and oil texture, restrained grain, elegant shapes, cinematic but intimate. Warm amber apartment light versus cool moon blue from screen, dark wood, subdued burgundy touches. Wry, creative, cozy mood with a little mystery, not horror or violent noir. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, important objects central enough for portrait thumbnail crop. Production quality, no decorative frame, no watermarks.

## otrada-mystery.png

Use case: illustration-story. A sophisticated painterly editorial illustration for chapters four to six of a contemporary Russian ironic detective novella about friends in an apartment and a missing USB flash drive. A small steel wall safe concealed behind a hinged decorative wooden kitchen shelf holding upright blue-and-white porcelain plates; shelf swings open as a disguised door, safe door open, the safe interior EMPTY. Below it a clean stainless-steel kitchen sink and a faucet with one glinting drop. In soft-focus foreground on the edge of a dark round wooden table: a faceted wooden pencil and a small receipt with indistinct marks (no readable text), cup of tea. Nobody visible. The safe, shelves and sink must form a believable domestic arrangement, no medieval setting. Narrative clue still life, wry cozy mystery and mild theatrical suspense. Rich painterly gouache/oil texture, muted amber practical light, cool deep blue shadows, dark walnut, discreet burgundy. Match a warm contemporary apartment series, not gritty crime horror. Landscape 3:2, central composition works for thumbnail crop. No text, no captions, no blood, guns, police badges, no watermark.

## otrada-farewell.png

Use case: illustration-story. Create a beautifully restrained painterly closing illustration for the Russian satirical detective novella 'Возвращение «Отрады»' by Uniform. Contemporary apartment, NOT a real medieval castle. Foreground in clear detail: on a round dark walnut table rest a small burgundy-and-black USB flash drive, an adjustable wrench and a modest ring of lock picks next to a folded white handkerchief; cups of unfinished tea. In soft-focus background an open apartment doorway leads into a cool dark corridor; an adult bearded man in an ordinary suit jacket is leaving, seen only from behind as a small distant silhouette. No identifiable real person. One empty chair pulled away from table conveys his absence. The story ends not with violence but with the talkative outsider leaving the friends after a mock trial; tone is humane, bittersweet, gently ironic, quiet loneliness after a farcical mystery. Warm amber lamplight inside meets moon-blue corridor, subtle burgundy accents, painterly oil and gouache texture matching a refined illustrated novella. Landscape 3:2, enough intimate room context, central clue objects and door visible, no giant hero, no extra people, no police, weapons, blood, texts, logos or watermark.
